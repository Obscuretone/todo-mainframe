TODO-74 MAINFRAME APP LIVE MVS 3270

Mainframe Todo App

Use this exact live 3270 flow. The app should show TODO-74 MAINFRAME MANAGEMENT V6.

  1. Install app: click Install/Refresh App and wait for the submitted message.
  2. Open logon: on the Hercules splash, click Reset Keyboard, then Open Logon Panel.
  3. User: click Send HERC01.
  4. Password: click Send CUL8TR.
  5. Welcome screens: click Continue / Enter until the TSO Applications menu appears.
  6. Reach READY: on the TSO Applications menu, click Exit Menu to READY.
  7. Start app: at READY, click Run Mainframe App. It calls the fresh TODO74 COBOL module.
  8. Browse todos: click First Todo, Next Todo, or Previous Todo. Each command shows one todo and returns to CMD.
  9. Manage todos: click Add Todo and type a title, or click Mark Done, Reopen, or Delete and type the shown todo ID.

Best logout: at READY, type LOGOFF and press Enter, then click Disconnect. If logon says USERID IN USE, click Force Drop Sessions; if MVS stays wedged, click Reboot Mainframe and wait about 90 seconds.
